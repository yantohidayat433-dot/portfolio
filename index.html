Vyzov Foundation.The Foundation for the
Development of Scientific
and Cultural Ties.

The foundation's mission is to reveal the names
of scientists whose achievements change lives for
the better, and to shape the perception of knowledge,
science, and technology as some of society's most
important values.

The foundation's mission is to reveal the names
of scientists whose achievements change lives
for the better, and to shape the perception of
knowledge, science, and technology as some of
society's most important values.

The foundation needed a unified visual identity that
would bring together the fund itself, its award, and
its exhibition projects under one visual language.

The foundation needed a unified visual identity
that would bring together the fund itself, its
award, and its exhibition projects under one
visual language.

Light became the central metaphor of the foundation's
visual identity, illuminating people, events, and
discoveries that usually stay out of sight. This idea runs
through all the graphics: a warm accent color emerges
from a deep, dark background, as if pulling its subjects
and their stories out of the dark like a spotlight.

Light became the central metaphor of the
foundation's visual identity, illuminating
people, events, and discoveries that usually
stay out of sight. This idea runs through all the
graphics: a warm accent color emerges from a
deep, dark background, as if pulling its subjects
and their stories out of the dark like a spotlight.

To express the connection between science and
culture, we chose contrasting typography: a strict
grotesque carries the precision and rationality of
science, while softer, more expressive accents speak
to the foundation's cultural, human side. Together,
they form a single but multifaceted visual voice.

To express the connection between science

expressive accents speak to the foundation's
